# Old arms of Nottingham — crest emblem

The device is the historic (oldest) coat of arms of the City of Nottingham —
a cross raguly between three open crowns. The design is centuries-old civic
heraldry and is in the public domain.

`public/assets/ui/crest/nottingham.svg` is a vector tracing of the 1898 arms
engraving reproduced in the 1905 "Date-Book" (nottshistory.org.uk,
images/nottingham/tts1905/arms1898.jpg), a pre-1928 work in the public domain.
Traced with potrace and recoloured to `currentColor` so it renders in ink or
wax. Used as the game's emblem stamp (HUD brand mark and the intro wax seal)
in place of the earlier letter monogram.
